Every run writes a normal log (participant playtest-…) that you can read below and under Results.

How to read the Stress-test list

  • red Fix before the study. The row says what to do.
  • orange Have a look; often fine.
  • green Nothing to do.
  • Waiting Not checked yet: press the button.
  • The robot plays every selected page while the story pretends a slow connection, a lost connection, blocked storage and a crash.
  • The other rows come from Regenerate and from the logs of real phones. To add a real phone, open the story on it with ?autoplay added to the address.
  • Only Plays to the end red? Run a Play-test: its log shows where the robot got stuck.
