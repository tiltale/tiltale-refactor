__TITLE__ — reset this device

Use this when this device still shows an old version of the story (for example old images after a republish). It removes everything this story stored in this browser: the downloaded pages and images, the offline copy, the progress and the participant ID. Other websites are not touched.

Do this while online, close other tabs with the story first, and repeat it in every browser on this device that was used for the story.

Open the story, or prepare it again for offline use.
