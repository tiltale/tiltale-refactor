__TITLE__ — prepare for offline use

For a classroom or field session without wifi: press the button while this device is online. Every page and image of the story is stored on this device. After that the story opens, restarts and replays without a connection; what happens is kept on the device and uploaded the next time it is online.

1 · Download the story onto this device

Do this on every device that will be used, and again after the story was republished. Images are stored in the size this device needs. Still seeing an old version? Open Reset this device first.

2 · After the session: upload what happened

Opening the story while online uploads automatically too; this button only makes it visible that nothing is left on the device.
